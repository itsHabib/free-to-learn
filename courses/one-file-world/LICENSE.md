## Algorithm credits

- The brightness curve uses Krzysztof Narkowicz's five-constant tone-mapping fit. He offers the fit under CC0 or MIT. [ACES Filmic Tone Mapping Curve](https://knarkowicz.wordpress.com/2016/01/06/aces-filmic-tone-mapping-curve/)
- The surf and wind use the coefficients of Paul Kellett's three-pole pink-noise approximation. [Pink noise filter](https://www.musicdsp.org/en/latest/Filters/76-pink-noise-filter.html)
- The seeded random generator uses Tommy Ettinger's Mulberry32 algorithm. He dedicated the generator to the public domain under CC0. [Mulberry32](https://gist.github.com/tommyettinger/46a874533244883189143505d203312c)
