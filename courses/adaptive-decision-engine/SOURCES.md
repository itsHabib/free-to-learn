# Sources and provenance

Beacon Keep mechanics, prose, exercises, diagrams, checks and synthetic events were authored for this course. Original SVG/CSS assets are included. The reference-to-render direction is restrained warm/cool contrast and clearly separated causal components; no reference images are distributed.

The local workflow adapts the accepted Tensor/Autograd course's preserved attempts, explicit earlier-layer support and positive export inventory. Its dependency-free stored ZIP writer and Markdown renderer were reused and extended within the same Ivy project. This is infrastructure reuse, not learner work or a new independent assessment.

Technical reading, checked 1 November 2026:

- Liquid d1 documentation: https://docs.liquid.ai/lfm/models/d1 — named typed request/response and optional raw HTTP boundary. Documentation support is not live qualification.
- Liquid decision models: https://docs.liquid.ai/lfm/models/decision-models — typed decisions. Vendor performance claims are not adopted as game evidence.
- Guo et al., On Calibration of Modern Neural Networks (2017): https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/guo17a.html — calibration concept. Our event, bins and fallback policy are original teaching choices.
- Agarwal et al., Deep Reinforcement Learning at the Edge of the Statistical Precipice (2021): https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2021/hash/f514cec81cb148559cf475e7426eed5e-Abstract.html — uncertainty in evaluation. Applying whole-episode pairing here is a design choice, not a validated sample-size guarantee.

Synthetic corpus: 60 development, 60 calibration and 60 evaluation episodes. Scores are explicitly authored and labels are computed from actual subsequent game transitions. Full source request/response/event records are in evaluation/corpus.json. Seeds are disjoint by split. No provider, credential or real user behavior is represented in this corpus.

Public minimums and timing estimates are course design choices. Human engagement, retention, learning gain, real-provider performance, a physical phone and screen-reader behavior remain unmeasured. Author qualification is source-aware; it is not independent learner assessment.

The repaired uncertainty method uses Hoeffding, W. (1963), “Probability Inequalities for Sums of Bounded Random Variables,” Theorem 2: https://www.cs.rpi.edu/academics/courses/spring06/random/hoefding.pdf . The two-sided bound is applied to independent episode summaries within a named domain; it is not a pooled row-level Wilson claim. Its assumptions and synthetic-data limitations are taught explicitly.
