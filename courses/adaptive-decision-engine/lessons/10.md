# 10 · A new pacing contract

The target now pulses, one round loses its pressure observation, and only two request reservations remain. Create your own director and defend which version should ship.

## Build without a finished project

Create project.mjs with an exported policy(state, memory) function. There is no completed capstone starter. You may compose your earlier modules, replace a heuristic, or keep an entirely rule-based policy. Return an action and serializable memory. Each returned decision must preserve origin and request count where applicable.

The public changed contract uses a pulse target: rounds alternate between lower and higher requested pressure bands in groups of three. One observation interval is unavailable. The action meanings, health limits and cooldown remain unchanged. Read the announced target rather than assuming steady values. A supplied targetSchedule gives twelve explicit intervals, while requestLimit gives the scenario allowance. On missing evidence, return a labelled rules fallback with no new request. The normal-state result must not pretend an outage occurred.

Prior-layer support is available as an explicit assisted route. It supplies the earlier modules, never project.mjs or your adoption conclusion. If you use it, retain your original draft and record the assistance. An assisted capstone can demonstrate integration, but it does not establish unaided mastery of the supplied layers.

## Freeze your experiment

Before evaluating the final version, save the baseline and candidate source identities, episode manifest, calibration identity, metric definitions and adoption limits in REPORT.md. State the hard gates: lawful execution, no duplicate or stale effects, complete event accounting and no more than two request reservations per episode. Choose allowed completion and survival losses and a target-distance improvement requirement using development data.

Run complete paired episodes. Report every bot/profile slice, including failures. Include an interval over paired episode differences. If the evidence remains ambiguous, choose rules or collect more evidence. You do not need a model win, a provider account or an optimistic conclusion to complete this project.

The public code check includes two final-round states with the same health and history but different announced targets. Charge 12 is already secured, every candidate survives both hazards, and there is no future round. Choose an action with the smallest worst-case band distance under the fixed player; every tied optimum is accepted. A health-only heuristic cannot satisfy both cases. This bounded final-step proof complements complete pulse/outage episodes; it does not require a greedy policy on earlier rounds. It also asks for meaningful game engagement: at least one completion and thirty percent challenge exposure across its twelve cases. Those are minimum artifact requirements, not proof of useful pacing. Your own frozen evaluation should be stronger and aligned with the product outcome you defend.

## Add an original scenario family

Create project-plan.json with your source fingerprint, adoption decision, rationale, limits, and two original scenario contracts. Each contract supplies its target schedule, outage rounds and request allowance. The checker executes the contracts and rejects two copies that differ only in name or seed. Run the same authored family from the evaluation panel or with node lab.mjs evaluate ./work --manifest ./work/project-plan.json --mode project to retain its full paired event log. Replace the REPORT.md starter with an evidence account; the code check establishes artifact completeness and consistency, while the separate rubric judges your reasoning. Create new seeds and a scenario family that exposes an assumption in your director. It may concentrate pressure near a target boundary, delay recovery until supplies are scarce, or change when observation is unavailable. Explain what stays inside the published game rules and what is a deliberate extension.

Preserve a controlled failure and repair using the method from module 08. Hold the original case fixed for the repair test, then test a different case that was not used to tune the repair. Label this public transfer practice honestly. Protected independent assessment requires another assessor to choose unseen cases after your submission is frozen; it is not included in the self-paced package.

The ordinary export includes your source and histories; place named result/checkpoint files through the supported commands described in API.md so they join the allowlist. ## Review the claim you are making

Use the six-part rubric: observation and constraints, baseline tradeoffs, uncertainty scope, paired evaluation, causal counterexample and bounded release decision. Each claim used to justify adoption needs supporting evidence. A high total cannot compensate for a false claim that a fixture was a real model run or that an illegal choice became valid because the executor repaired it.

State what remains untested. The supplied fixture comparison does not qualify Liquid or any other provider. Automated public checks do not prove independent transfer. A source-aware walkthrough does not become a blind learner trial. Human learning, motivation and full accessibility require corresponding human observations.

A successful outcome is a working director with a defensible choice about its operating policy. If your evidence says the rules baseline should ship, deliver that decision with the same care as a selective model policy. The course is about controlling and measuring decisions, including the decision not to delegate one.
