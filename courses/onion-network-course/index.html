Circuit Observatory · Local relay lab

Build your own local onion-routing system

Write the client and relay protocol. Create an encrypted circuit, carry a fictional lookup through three hops, stop a relay and recover with fresh keys.

Real processes, local IPC. Four separate Node relay processes exchange messages through a trusted local parent. TCP and multiple machines are not qualified. This is an educational prototype, not Tor or a real anonymity service.
