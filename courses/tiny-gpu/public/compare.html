How this page draws the scene

The lab sent the scene's triangles, already in clip coordinates, and your picture. This page draws the triangles twice with WebGL2, antialiasing off: once for colour and once with each triangle's number as its colour, so the lab can tell which triangle owns each pixel. First it draws three small scenes whose edges pass exactly through pixel centres, to find which tie rule this GPU uses, and a scene of thin triangles whose coverage depends on how finely the GPU snaps vertex positions. WebGL promises 1/16 of a pixel or finer; the lab draws your picture on the grid this GPU really uses. The pixels go back to the lab, which compares them with yours and prints the verdict.
