Frame Perfect

Step Kettle into a wall, one frame at a time

Kettle is the course's small platformer. Every picture on this page is the game itself, run from the same code the course's checks run.

1. The wall

This is level 2, frame 63 of a run the course's search found. Kettle is in the air, moving right at 6 px a frame, toward a wall one tile (8 px) thick that it is too low to jump over.

What happens when Kettle reaches the wall?

Read from the game's RAM after each frame
FrameButtonsx (px)vx (px a frame)

x is Kettle's left edge; Kettle is 6 px wide. The wall covers x 272 to 279.

2. Beat par by hand

Level 1, par 120 frames. Hold buttons with the pads below, then play in real time or step one frame at a time, the way tool-assisted runs are made. While focus is on these controls, the left and right arrow keys hold left and right, and the up arrow holds jump.

Frame 0. Par 120.

Tap a pad to hold it and tap again to let go. Going back a frame is what speedrunners call a rerecord: 0 so far.
