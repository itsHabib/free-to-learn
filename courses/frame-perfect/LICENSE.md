## Algorithm credits

- The random generator is a 16-bit xorshift generator with the shift triple (7, 9, 8), from the family George Marsaglia described. [Xorshift RNGs, Journal of Statistical Software, 2003](https://www.jstatsoft.org/article/view/v008i14)
- The state hash is FNV-1a, by Glenn Fowler, Landon Curt Noll and Kiem-Phong Vo, which its authors placed in the public domain. [FNV hash](http://www.isthe.com/chongo/tech/comp/fnv/)
- The A* search is the algorithm of Peter Hart, Nils Nilsson and Bertram Raphael. [A Formal Basis for the Heuristic Determination of Minimum Cost Paths, 1968](https://doi.org/10.1109/TSSC.1968.300136)
- The movie minimiser follows the idea of Andreas Zeller's delta debugging, in its simplest one-change-at-a-time form. [Simplifying and Isolating Failure-Inducing Input, 2002](https://doi.org/10.1109/32.988498)
